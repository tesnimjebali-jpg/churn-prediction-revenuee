Churn Prediction & Revenue Impact
Project Overview

This project predicts customer churn and estimates the associated financial impact for a telecommunications company.

The objective is not only to identify customers who are likely to leave, but also to estimate how much revenue the company could potentially lose and prioritize the customers who should receive retention actions first.

The project combines:

Data preprocessing
Exploratory Data Analysis
Machine Learning classification
Model evaluation
False Positive / False Negative analysis
Customer financial value estimation
Revenue-at-risk calculation
Customer prioritization
Business recommendations
Interactive Streamlit dashboard
Business Problem

Customer churn can create significant revenue loss.

A company therefore needs to answer two main questions:

Which customers are most likely to leave?
How much money could the company lose if these customers churn?

This project connects Machine Learning predictions with a business decision-making process.

False Positive vs False Negative

A False Positive occurs when the model predicts that a customer will churn, but the customer actually stays.

Possible consequence:

The company may unnecessarily offer a discount or retention incentive.

A False Negative occurs when the model predicts that a customer will stay, but the customer actually leaves.

Possible consequence:

The company fails to intervene and may lose the revenue generated by that customer.

For this project, False Negatives are considered more costly.

Therefore, Recall is an important business metric, because higher Recall allows the company to identify more real churners.

Customer Financial Value

The customer financial value is estimated using:

CustomerValue = MonthlyCharges × 12

This represents an estimated customer revenue value over the next twelve months.

Financial Risk Score

The financial risk associated with each customer is calculated using:

RiskScore = P(Churn) × CustomerValue

In the project, this value is stored as:

EstimatedLoss

This approach allows the company to move from:

Who is likely to churn?

to:

Which customers represent the greatest potential financial loss?

Revenue at Risk

The estimated total revenue at risk calculated by the project is:

$1,679,422.41

This represents the sum of the expected financial losses associated with customer churn probabilities.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Machine Learning can be connected directly to business decision-making.

Instead of only predicting whether a customer will churn, the solution estimates the financial impact of each customer and creates a prioritized retention list.

The final system therefore provides both:

Predictive insight
Financial decision support

This allows the company to focus retention efforts on the customers whose departure would create the greatest business impact.
